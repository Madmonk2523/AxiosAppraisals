About Axios Appraisals

Deep-Rooted Local Knowledge.
Uncompromising Accuracy.

Axios Appraisals is a certified real estate appraisal firm serving clients across all five boroughs and counties in New York and New Jersey. With an intimate understanding of local neighborhoods and market conditions, we deliver valuations grounded in real, current market data.

Our appraisals are relied upon by homeowners, attorneys, financial institutions, and courts. Whether you need a fair market value assessment for an estate settlement, a neutral valuation during a divorce proceeding, or a private property report for your own planning, we approach every engagement with rigor, confidentiality, and professionalism.

We handle all property types — residential single-family homes, condominiums, co-ops, multi-family buildings, and mixed-use properties — bringing the same level of diligence to every appraisal regardless of scope.
